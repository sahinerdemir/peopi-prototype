Logo usage rules — initial proposal

Keep the symbol unwarped, with generous clear space. Avoid gradients inside the glyph, heavy effects, and placing it on busy photography without contrast. The Welcome screen uses a typographic p. concept; final custom vector geometry, optical spacing, minimum size, and app-icon export remain to be approved.

The existing workspace sidebar also uses a separate abstract SVG mark. This guide treats p. as the chosen brand direction, not as a finalized vector asset. The two marks should be reconciled before release.
02 / Palette

Color & atmosphere.

Click a swatch to copy HEX

Orange communicates action and discovery. Ink and muted neutrals carry reading, surfaces and hierarchy. Semantic status colors must remain distinct from visibility state; color must never be the only status signal.

Light mode

Light canvas, white cards, soft blue-gray borders and strong dark typography.

Dark mode

Deep ink canvas, raised slate surfaces, softened borders and bright readable type.

06 / Motion

Motion with purpose.

Ambient

Welcome orbit breathing uses slow 7s / 9s cycles. Decorative motion must not distract from actions.

Interaction

Proposed: 150–250ms for controls and 250–400ms for panels. Prefer opacity and transform; respect reduced-motion settings.

07 / Experience

Privacy is a design rule.

Invisible until Open

Closed mode never exposes a person’s live presence or precise location.

Hide ≠ Block

Hiding presence preserves a connection and messaging. Blocking is a separate, stronger action.

Truthful activity

Do not fabricate nearby people, activity zones or verified status. Prototype content must be labeled as demo.

Accessibility baseline: meaningful focus states, readable text, adequate contrast, 44px-ish touch targets where practical, keyboard support and reduced-motion alternatives. These are targets, not claims of completed compliance.
